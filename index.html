How it works

Default: Ping

Ping is PingPals' own group chat. Everyone who picks Default chats together in Ping, no other app needed.

Translator

The Bridge Bot turns on. Everyone in Translation mode sees every message, with a tag showing which app it came from, so friends on different apps can talk together.

Online dots

Every person has an icon with a dot on the bottom left. Green is online, yellow means away or AFK (no typing for 30 seconds, or the page is hidden), and gray means they left.

AFK Game

Friends offline? Battle RoastBot 3000. You have 30 seconds to roast the robot each round. Good roasts drain its battery. Weak roasts or running out of time cost you a life. Lose all 3 lives and RoastBot wins.

RPG mode

Opens the game Demon Conqueror in a new tab. While you play, your friends see "playing Demon Conqueror ⚔️" next to your name, so they know where you are.

Animate Image

Drag in a picture and describe how it should move, like "melt like ice cream with sparkles." The whole picture animates with effects like melting, waves, swirls, glitches, and fire. Then download it as a GIF.

Watch a video

Paste a YouTube link or type what you want to watch, and it opens on YouTube. Friends see "watching a video 📺" next to your name.

Message alerts

Playing a game or in a different chat? When a friend sends a message, an alert pops up at the top. Tap it to jump straight to that chat.

Typing indicator

When someone is writing, you see bouncing dots and their name. If the dots stop and nothing arrives, they might be AFK or doing something else.
